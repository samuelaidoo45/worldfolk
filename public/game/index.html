AKWAABA TO YOUR NEW LIFE

Live your Accra story.

Choose your starting place on the city map and enter Accra with ₵100,000. Explore the streets, find work, meet people and make this city your own. Register or sign in before entering the world. Your profile keeps your home, money and belongings across devices.

Your @handle is visible to players. Tap a handle to view their profile and chat.
HOW TO PLAY

A day in Accra

  1. Live with I or My life. Every new character starts with ₵100,000. Choose a public place on the opening map to start exploring. A nearby home is kept for your character. Apply for work, complete shifts, buy goods, cook groceries and furnish your home. Private homes need an owner-issued guest pass.
  2. Roam with W A S D, arrow keys or the touch joystick. Hold Shift to run and use Q / R to turn. On touchscreens, use the left joystick to move, tap Run to switch pace, and drag the centre of the screen to look around. Portrait and landscape are both supported. Open ☰ for destinations, transport, camera settings and help. Hold the right mouse button and drag to look around; K changes camera distance.
  3. Enter buildings at glowing doors with E or Interact. Walk inside, use furnishings, and return through the exit.
  4. Drive nearby vehicles with F or the vehicle button. Buy a motorcycle in Shops and select it from Transport. Book airplane journeys at the marked game terminals. W accelerates, S brakes and reverses, A / D steer, and Space is the handbrake. Press V to see the driver's seat. Park and press F or E to get out.
  5. Explore with N to choose a destination. While driving, choose Auto-drive 4×, Drive myself, or Skip to arrival before selecting a place. Change auto-drive speed up to 8× on the route card; steer or brake to take over. Stop and press F to park. M opens the full city map. Game sounds can be muted in ☰; H honks while driving.
  6. Connect by tapping a player’s @handle to view their profile, add them as a friend or send a private message. Open Friends & messages in the menu or chat panel to find conversations. Use C or Chat for city chat. Use Voice chat to join the live voice circle; hold B to talk. T requests taxi or tro-tro pickup; K switches street/scenic cameras. Reverse away from obstacles or steer along them. Repeated vehicle theft attracts the police. Computer-controlled residents also walk the streets: approach one and press E to talk.
