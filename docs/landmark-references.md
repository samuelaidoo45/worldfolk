# Accra landmark reconstruction references

The outdoor landmark registry is geographically anchored independently of tile
visibility. Source IDs, coordinates and grandstand envelopes come from the bundled
OpenStreetMap extract. These are authored approximations of photographed exteriors,
not surveyed models or photogrammetry. Reference photographs are not redistributed.

- Black Star Gate: https://cdn.natuerlich.reisen/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/Ghana-Accra-1920x1080.jpg — pale stone, three openings, cornice, parapet, black star, inscriptions, paired fountains.
- Independence Square aerial: https://www.santanamigos.com/san.ven.blackstarsquare.480x640.jpg — distinct curved Independence Arch at the coast; open grandstands surround the parade ground.
- Ghana Tourism Authority: https://visitghana.com/independence-square/ — distinguishes Black Star Gate in the northern roundabout from Independence Arch south of the square.
- Accra Mall: https://www.accramall.com/accra-mall-about — curved metal entrance canopy and low retail complex.
- Balme Library: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Balme_Library.JPG — cream walls, red pitched roofs and clock tower.
- National Mosque: https://mosqpedia.org/masajid/ghana-national-mosque/ — central dome, subsidiary domes and four minarets.

Other buildings retain OSM footprints and available height/material tags, with
procedural façades where reference assets are unavailable. This does not reproduce
every Accra property or its private interior exactly. Authored assets need to be
extended per named landmark; changing a category's colour is not a reconstruction.

## Expanded named registry (October 2026)

`popular-landmarks-data.js` is generated by `scripts/build-landmark-registry.mjs`.
It preserves OSM building IDs and rings; authored visual heights are estimates.
Complexes use their mapped perimeter and courtyards rather than a rectangular
replacement. The stadium model replaces the mapped west grandstand only; other
stadium structures and the pitch retain their mapped positions. Kempinski's model
currently covers one mapped hotel wing, not a surveyed reconstruction of the site.

Additional exterior reference sources:

- National Theatre, architect-supplied description and photography: https://www.archdaily.com/891153/ghana-national-theatre-cctn-design — three interlocking curved white volumes.
- Ghana National Mosque, Architects Registration Council: https://www.arc.gov.gh/portfolios/ghana-national-mosque-complex/ — Ottoman domes, minarets and arched openings.
- Nkrumah Mausoleum, HACSA Foundation: https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/the-mausoleum-hacsa-foundation/dQEQZS7XbCr7PQ — upward-curving marble ribs and central chamber.
- Jamestown Lighthouse, Ghana Tourism guide: https://visitghana.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Ghana-MICE-Guide-4.pdf — red and white tower, lantern gallery.
- Kaneshie Market, local assembly repository: https://ghanadistricts.com/Home/ReaderDistrict/d78fef7-3768-4c67-b5 — yellow open market façade.
- National Museum: https://mainlymuseums.com/post/1251/national-museum-of-ghana/ — low modernist dome and glazed band.
- Osu Castle, Ghana Museums and Monuments Board: https://gmmb.gov.gh/forts-and-castles/ — white coastal fort, red roofs and towers.
- Jubilee House: https://www.ghanaconsulate.org.nz/uploads/5/6/7/8/56787757/jubilee-house_orig.jpg — stool-inspired sweeping roof and screened façades.
- Balme Library photographic collection: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Balme_Library — tiled wings and clock tower.
- Accra Sports Stadium aerial: https://www.siamsporttalk.com/en/olympic-2/venue/147.html — stepped coloured seating and covered grandstand.

Marina Mall, the cathedral and hotel models remain schematic interpretations of
mapped footprints and identifiable architectural features. No claim of measured
accuracy or photorealistic scanning is made. Uncatalogued places continue to use
map-driven architecture; this registry is intentionally extensible.

Vehicles now use distinct sedan, hatchback, SUV, pickup, van, taxi and bus bodies,
wheel arches, window pillars, mirrors, plates and clearcoat materials. Their
collision envelope is kept consistent with the existing traffic simulation.
