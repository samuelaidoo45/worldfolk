# Accra landmark reconstruction references

The outdoor landmark registry is geographically anchored independently of tile
visibility. Source IDs, coordinates and grandstand envelopes come from the bundled
OpenStreetMap extract. These are authored approximations of photographed exteriors,
not surveyed models or photogrammetry. Reference photographs are not redistributed.

- Black Star Gate: https://cdn.natuerlich.reisen/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/Ghana-Accra-1920x1080.jpg — pale stone, three openings, cornice, parapet, black star, inscriptions, paired fountains.
- Independence Square aerial: https://www.santanamigos.com/san.ven.blackstarsquare.480x640.jpg — distinct curved Independence Arch at the coast; open grandstands surround the parade ground.
- Ghana Tourism Authority: https://visitghana.com/independence-square/ — distinguishes Black Star Gate in the northern roundabout from Independence Arch south of the square.
- Accra Mall: https://www.accramall.com/accra-mall-about — curved metal entrance canopy and low retail complex.
- Balme Library: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Balme_Library.JPG — cream walls, red pitched roofs and clock tower.
- National Mosque: https://mosqpedia.org/masajid/ghana-national-mosque/ — central dome, subsidiary domes and four minarets.

Other buildings retain OSM footprints and available height/material tags, with
procedural façades where reference assets are unavailable. This does not reproduce
every Accra property or its private interior exactly. Authored assets need to be
extended per named landmark; changing a category's colour is not a reconstruction.

## Expanded named registry (October 2026)

`popular-landmarks-data.js` is generated by `scripts/build-landmark-registry.mjs`.
It preserves OSM building IDs and rings; authored visual heights are estimates.
Complexes use their mapped perimeter and courtyards rather than a rectangular
replacement. The stadium model replaces the mapped west grandstand only; other
stadium structures and the pitch retain their mapped positions. Kempinski's model
currently covers one mapped hotel wing, not a surveyed reconstruction of the site.

Marina Mall, the cathedral and hotel models remain schematic interpretations of
mapped footprints and identifiable architectural features. No claim of measured
accuracy or photorealistic scanning is made. Uncatalogued places continue to use
map-driven architecture; this registry is intentionally extensible.

Vehicles now use distinct sedan, hatchback, SUV, pickup, van, taxi and bus bodies,
wheel arches, window pillars, mirrors, plates and clearcoat materials. Their
collision envelope is kept consistent with the existing traffic simulation.

## Independence precinct — 11 October 2026 reconstruction pass

Reference review: [HACSA Foundation's Independence Square exhibit](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/independence-square-hacsa-foundation/5QUhVVravCqiqw?hl=en), including the frontal photograph of the seaward arch and stands; [Black Star Gate photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Black_Star_Monument,_Accra,_Ghana.JPG).

The gate now has three portals, stone courses, circular medallions, a coffered
soffit, a recessed date panel and a solid star spanning the raised central block.
The separate seaward arch has two open decks, railings, a front black star and
stair flights. Its invented gate inscriptions were removed. The eight mapped
stands have open bent supports, stepped terraces, bench bays, stair aisles and
red/gold/green frontal fascia. Geometry is batched by material.

Photographs were used as visual reference only and are not shipped as textures.
Positions and stand orientations retain the OSM extract. Dimensions and details
are approximations, not a measured survey. The square stays open at ground level;
portals are collision-free, while piers, fountains, stair bases and seating
terraces have solid boundaries. Vertical stair climbing, platform access, event
crowds, the Unknown Soldier Memorial and detailed connecting streets remain
separate acceptance work. This pass does not mark the entire precinct complete.

## Unknown Soldier Memorial and precinct circulation — 11 October 2026

- Ghana Tourism Authority: https://visitghana.com/independence-square/ — memorial opposite the Independence Arch; 28th February Road and the separate Black Star Gate.
- Boris Kester's photographed south-facing memorial view: https://www.traveladventures.org/continents/africa/accra-independence-square.html and https://www.traveladventures.org/countries/ghana/images/accra-independence-square05.jpg — stepped dark stone platform, tall narrow plinth, standing bronze sentry, ceremonial bowl and flag rows. Viewed as a reference; the photograph is not redistributed.
- Bundled OSM POI 1055196850, labelled “Liberation Day Monument”, anchors the model at [-0.1927328, 5.54826075]. Search accepts this mapped name as well as Unknown Soldier Memorial.
- `precinct-streets-data.js` contains 87 exact road/path lines from the bundled OSM extract (ODbL). Kerbs and edge markings follow those lines, with gaps at junction mouths. Widths, arrows, kerbs and paving details are game interpretations, not a surveyed road-works inventory.
- Memorial dimensions and surface detail remain photo-based approximations. Arch access routes have been adapted for connected playable stairs and adequate head clearance; they are not an architectural survey or a claim that these platforms are publicly accessible in real life.
