## Work in coherent blocks

Several verified neighbouring addresses can be implemented in one pass, with the street, ground, entrances and activity spaces connecting them. Each address still needs its own native ID, references, status and accuracy limits. Shared proximity alone does not justify copying one generic facade or interior across the block.

Before modelling, inspect current photographs, operator sources and available Google Street View; record actual capture dates and missing views. Do not treat publication/upload dates as capture dates. Where evidence is missing, keep native geometry and record the gap.

For each block:

- Match massing, roof, frontage and signage to their references and actual use. Defer new interior modelling to the second phase.
- Distinguish asphalt, grey paving, bare ground and grass from evidence; include verified drains, kerbs, crossings, lighting and vegetation.
- Provide usable outdoor entrances, exits, public seats and appropriate outdoor people/activity. Detailed indoor rooms and activities belong to the later interior phase.
- Keep driveways, bus approaches and pedestrian paths clear; check vehicle queues, collisions and camera clearance.
- Verify the connected visit in the running game and record evidence, remaining accuracy gaps and local/deployed state.
