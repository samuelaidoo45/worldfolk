## What the numbers mean

These are **project inventory counts**, not an audited census of Accra and not a measure of realistic completion. The extract spans longitude −0.40 to 0.12 and latitude 5.40 to 5.83. Municipal boundaries and current business operation have not been verified for every record. Branch families and whole districts must be divided into individual places before modelling.

| Inventory | Current records | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Place-search entries | 12,038 | Includes branches, businesses, landmarks and area labels; possible real-world duplicates remain |
| Street-search entries | 6,209 | Searchable names/representative points, not full roads |
| Building footprints | 664,218 | Geometry records, not separate households; only 2,821 have names in this extract |

## How to update one place

1. **Choose a bounded unit.** Select a work ID and an address, building, compound or road section. Split “bank branches,” “homes” or “market district” into child IDs such as `ACC-036-001`; keep the parent as an address family. Never count parent and children as distinct completed locations.
2. **Link records.** Add the mapped place ID, building/road IDs, coordinates and surveyed boundary to its reference card. Names alone are insufficient; nearby businesses can share names.
3. **Collect references.** Front, side, roof, ground, street approach and publicly available interior references, with dates and source links. Identify measured dimensions versus estimates and authored interiors.
4. **Build the complete visit.** Exterior and ground, entry/interior, furniture, people, sounds, an activity, a work/purchase loop where appropriate, then departure. Public spaces use **Not applicable** for interiors where justified.
5. **Check behaviour.** Walking/running, vehicle arrival, collisions, stairs, continuous sitting/sleeping, reconnect/save, nearby players and camera framing.
6. **Review in context.** Day/night screenshots, phone portrait/landscape and desktop, recorded frame times on named devices. Compare recognizable features with the reference, including neighbouring street context.
7. **Record the update.** Edit status, seven stage fields, next action, evidence and reviewed date in `coverage-work-items.csv`. Link the relevant neighbourhood and raw map records. Record commit/release and remaining issues in `implementation-progress.md`.
8. **Accept explicitly.** Only move to **Accepted** after the full visit and visual/device/multiplayer checks are recorded. A successful deploy or existing procedural shell is not full acceptance.

### Status vocabulary

- **Queued:** work scoped at candidate level; current references or address breakdown still needed.
- **Research:** references or placement being gathered; model work not yet established by this tracker.
- **In progress:** a partial implementation exists, with recorded remaining work.
- **Ready for review:** implementation ready for the complete visit and visual acceptance checks.
- **Accepted:** all applicable acceptance evidence recorded for the defined unit.
- **Blocked:** record the concrete missing input and next action.
- **Expansion:** map boundary/data/routing expansion must be scoped first.

Stage fields use **Not assessed**, **Research**, **Partial**, **Verified** and **Not applicable**. Do not calculate a city completion percentage from unequal packages or area labels. Count accepted addresses/blocks separately from shared systems and aggregate families.
